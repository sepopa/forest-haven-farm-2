No. Every loaf is leavened only with our wild sourdough starter, which has been maintained since 2016. No commercial yeast, dough conditioners, or preservatives go into any loaf.

Not currently. All of our bread is made with wheat and/or rye flour, so it isn't suitable for gluten-free or celiac diets.

All loaves contain wheat. Some contain dairy, honey, or nuts/seeds (see the Menu page for details on each item). Let us know about any allergy in the notes field when you order and we'll flag it before baking.

Store cut-side down at room temperature in a paper or bread bag for up to 4 days, or freeze whole/sliced for up to 3 months. A few minutes in a hot oven brings back that fresh-baked crust.
