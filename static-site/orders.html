Before You Order

  • 48-hour lead time. Orders placed after Wednesday may roll to the following week's bake.
  • Two pickup spots. The farm stand (Fri & Sat, 9am–1pm) or our Saturday farmers market table.
  • Payment at pickup. We accept cash, card, and Venmo — nothing is charged online.
  • Confirmation. We'll email or call within 24 hours to confirm your order and exact pickup time.
